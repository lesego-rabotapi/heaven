Story

A Second Chance,
A Cup of Coffee.

Mondli Mahamba opened Heaven Coffee in April 2017 inside the Central Methodist Mission on Greenmarket Square — one of Cape Town's most storied buildings, and one of the city's most recognisable church facades.

The café grew from a belief that community and coffee belong together. Inside Gothic stone arches, next to wooden pews, Heaven became a place of welcome for everyone who steps off the square — locals on a morning commute, tourists looking for somewhere quiet, and everyone in between.

"Second chances and community — those are the foundations."
